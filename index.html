Who We Are

A team revolutionizing the stone business

Heritage Natural Stone Company was built to change a previously slow-moving industry. We exist to assist you in choice and get you high-quality stone faster than anyone else.

Backed by our parent company’s 25-year track record and over 2,000 successful supply orders, we have mastered every step of the stone supply chain. Our ambitious, young workforce brings energy, speed and modern thinking to every order.

Traditional stone expertise,

This ensures we don’t simply offer what is available, but find what is truly right for your project. The materials we select today become part of what we leave behind.
